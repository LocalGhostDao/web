You run it on hardware you own, so you hold its keys and its data, and nobody else can recover them. In wisp 0.0.1 there's no recovery path, so a dead board or a forgotten PIN means the archive on that disk is gone, and a wipe PIN destroys everything on purpose. Keep the originals of anything you can't lose somewhere else as well. The known gaps of each release are listed in its notes, and the changelog keeps the dates.

> 3. THE SETUP MIRROR

Every file on the setup mirror is published under the terms it came with, and those terms sit beside it in the same folder as TERMS-<name>.txt, with a NOTICE.txt saying which file is under which. Some of them ask things of you. OpenStreetMap data is under the Open Database License, which asks for attribution and for derived databases to stay open. EmbeddingGemma is under the Gemma Terms of Use, whose use restrictions pass to anyone you give it to. The Copernicus elevation data asks for its attribution to be kept. The mirror itself is offered as is, may change or go away, and is signed so that a copy anyone else hosts can be checked.
