Whisper large-v3-turbo , OpenAI (weights) and the whisper.cpp maintainers (ggml conversion)

ggml-large-v3-turbo-q5_0.bin is an unmodified copy of the file published at
https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp, converted to the ggml format used by whisper.cpp and
quantised to q5_0 from OpenAI's Whisper large-v3-turbo weights
(https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo). The conversion and the quantisation are the
whisper.cpp maintainers'; LocalGhost changes nothing further.
