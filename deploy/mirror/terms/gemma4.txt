Gemma 4 , Google (weights) and Unsloth (GGUF quantisation)

The Gemma 4 GGUF files on this mirror are unmodified copies of files Unsloth published on Hugging
Face, converted and quantised by Unsloth from Google's Gemma 4 instruction-tuned weights. NOTICE.txt
beside each file names the exact upstream file.

  gemma-4-12b-it-Q4_K_M.gguf and mmproj-F16.gguf (set "models"), from
  https://huggingface.co/unsloth/gemma-4-12b-it-GGUF, quantised from google/gemma-4-12B-it

  gemma-4-E2B-it-qat-UD-Q2_K_XL.gguf (set "phone"), from
  https://huggingface.co/unsloth/gemma-4-E2B-it-qat-GGUF, quantised from Google's quantisation-aware
  checkpoint google/gemma-4-E2B-it-qat-q4_0-unquantized

The quantisations modify the original weights (Apache 2.0, section 4(b)); the modifications are
Unsloth's, and LocalGhost changes nothing further.
