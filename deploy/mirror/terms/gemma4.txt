Gemma 4 , Google (weights) and Unsloth (GGUF quantisation)

gemma-4-12b-it-Q4_K_M.gguf and mmproj-F16.gguf are unmodified copies of the GGUF files Unsloth
published at https://huggingface.co/unsloth/gemma-4-12b-it-GGUF, converted and quantised by Unsloth
from Google's Gemma 4 12B instruction-tuned weights (https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-12B-it).
The Q4_K_M quantisation modifies the original weights (Apache 2.0, section 4(b)); the modification
is Unsloth's, and LocalGhost changes nothing further.
