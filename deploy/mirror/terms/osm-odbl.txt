The tiles are a derivative database of the OpenStreetMap land polygons published by
https://osmdata.openstreetmap.de (land-polygons-complete-4326): each polygon clipped to a one-degree
cell and its coordinates rounded to 1/65535 of a degree, by internal/landtiles in the LocalGhost
repository (https://github.com/LocalGhostDao). This derivative database is offered under the same
licence, the ODbL 1.0; the method that produced it is that source code.
