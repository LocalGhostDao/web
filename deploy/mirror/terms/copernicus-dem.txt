Copernicus DEM GLO-90 , the Copernicus WorldDEM-90

The .tif tiles and tileList.txt in this set are unmodified copies of the Copernicus DEM GLO-90 from the
public copy on the AWS Registry of Open Data (https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/), one
Cloud Optimised GeoTIFF per 1x1 degree tile.
