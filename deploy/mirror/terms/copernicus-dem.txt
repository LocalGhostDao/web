Copernicus DEM GLO-90 , the Copernicus WorldDEM-90

The data in this set is the Copernicus DEM GLO-90 from the public copy on the AWS Registry of Open
Data (https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/), one Cloud Optimised GeoTIFF per 1x1 degree tile,
and tileList.txt is upstream's list of those tiles, unmodified. The tiles are carried in .heights packs,
one per 30-degree block (GLO-90_N30_W030.heights is 30 to 60 north, 30 west to 0): each pack is the
block's tiles with their bytes exactly as published behind a small index, made with ghost-heights
from the LocalGhost release named in NOTICE.txt. Nothing in a tile is changed, so the data is as
Copernicus published it.
